# Module 2 review sheet: heredity

## Vocabulary to know

- Gene, allele, chromosome
- Dominant and recessive
- Genotype and phenotype
- Homozygous and heterozygous
- Incomplete dominance and codominance

## Practice problems

1. In guinea pigs, black fur (B) is dominant over white fur (b). What fraction of the offspring of a Bb x bb cross is expected to have white fur?
2. Two pink four o'clock flowers are crossed. List the expected phenotypes of the offspring and their ratio.
3. A mother has type A blood with genotype IAi and a father has type B blood with genotype IBi. Which blood types are possible for their children?
